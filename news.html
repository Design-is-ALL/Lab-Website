2026Oct 17

Invited talk at Cal State LA

Dr. Huang was invited to speak at Cal State LA in the Seminar in Interdisciplinary STEM Research, supported by the NSF CREST Center for Advancement toward Sustainable Urban Systems (CATSUS). Her talk, “Designing Learning-based Autonomous Systems for Complex Engineering Tasks,” showed how multi-agent reinforcement learning and social learning help teams of robots and autonomous agents self-organize, coordinate, and adapt to complex tasks. Afterward she talked with students and faculty about research opportunities in intelligent systems.

2026Sept

Lab’s trustworthy AI project featured by CSUF News

CSUF News featured the lab’s Honda-supported project, “Building Trust in Human–AI Robotics Teaming for the Future Manufacturing Workforce.” Twenty students were selected for the one-year project. They are building a simulated manufacturing environment where people assign tasks to AI-driven robots and observe how the robots respond, so that trust can be modeled, measured, and designed for. Trustworthy-AI concepts will also be brought into existing engineering courses, reaching more than 200 students. The story features lab graduate researcher Rahul Lunagariya.

2026Aug

Lab presents at ASME IDETC-CIE 2026, Houston

Lab undergraduate researcher Evan Grzeszkiewicz-O’Neill presented the paper “Learning to Represent Social Information in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Self-Organized Systems” at the 2026 ASME International Design Engineering Technical Conferences and Computers and Information in Engineering Conference (IDETC-CIE) in Houston, Texas. The work studies how agents can encode information learned from their teammates to improve coordination in self-organizing systems.
